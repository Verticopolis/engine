The .TDT byte map

Every byte of a SimTower .TDT save, in file order: what we know, what we only suspect and what nobody has decoded yet. This page owns the layout: every offset, size, field and grade. tdt-format.md owns the meaning (unit types, the original’s rules, what the codec does) and links here instead of restating a table. The two must agree. Where they differ, fix both in the same change, and keep a layout fact in one place only.

Snapshot. Written 2026-10-30 from the nine game-written saves in conformance/fixtures/tdt-reddit/, the game’s own re-saves of our exports (pc-verify/ground-truth in the project files) and the public specs. It is a snapshot of what we knew that day. The live evidence is the fixtures plus tests/tdt_real_saves.rs; re-measure there before trusting a “constant in every save” claim for a save the nine do not cover.

Sources. [OS] OpenSkyscraper’s notes, [TD] dfloer/tower-docs (tdt_spec.md, CC-BY-SA 4.0), [RE] measured by us on the nine saves. No code or text from either upstream is copied, and the original game’s source and binaries were not read (clean room, see tdt-format.md).

Confidence.

  • Known: seen in every save and checked against the game’s own display or a harness re-save.
  • Inferred: consistent across all nine saves, meaning deduced from the values; not confirmed in the game.
  • Unknown: the bytes are mapped (we know where they are and how they vary) but not what they mean. Listed with the values seen.

The nine saves: TOWER1 to TOWER8 and MYTOWER (u/invokes). Counts below that say “all nine” count those files only. Offsets inside a block are decimal from the block start unless written 0x.

All integers are little-endian. After the floor map nothing sits at a fixed offset: walk the blocks in order.

1. Whole file, in order

Section titled “1. Whole file, in order”
# Block Size (bytes) Notes
1 Header 560 66 bytes used, the rest zero in all nine saves
2 Floors 0 to 119 6 + 18 n + 188 each n = unit count of that floor
3 People 4 + 16 N N is a pool size (multiple of 256 in game saves)
4 Retail table 9,216 512 rows of 18
5 Elevator slots 0 to 23 194 empty, 194 + 720 + 324 k + 2,768 built k = floor entries (section 6)
6 Statistic words 88 22 x i32
7 Finance 132 33 x i32
8 Undecoded flags 12
9 Undecoded bytes 42
10 Parking 1,026 u16 count + 512 x u16
11 Clinic list 22 u16 count + 10 x u16
12 Stairs and escalators 640 64 x 10
13 Tail 17,242 (+48 with named units) sparse routing state, section 8
14 Our stamp 7 VCTDT + u16, exports only; never part of the 1994 file

Every offset in blocks 6 to 12 is fixed relative to the end of block 5 (call it F): statistic words F+0, finance F+88, flags F+220, bytes F+232, parking F+274, clinic list F+1,300, stairs F+1,322, tail F+1,962.

Measured example, TOWER2.TDT (470,530 bytes): people at 68,282 with N = 9,984; retail at 228,030; elevators at 237,246 (214,080 bytes for 24 slots); statistic words at 451,326; finance at 451,414; parking at 451,600; clinic list at 452,626; stairs at 452,648; tail at 453,288 (17,242 bytes).

2. Header (560 bytes)

Section titled “2. Header (560 bytes)”

Only bytes 0 to 65 are ever non-zero in the nine saves. Bytes 66 to 559 are zero in all of them (no field known there).

Off Size Field Values seen Meaning Status
0 u16 magic 0x2400 File signature Known
2 u16 level 1 to 6 Star rating; 6 = TOWER Known
4 i32 balance Funds; shown x100 Known
8 i32 otherIncome 5,000 in TOWER1, else 0 Finance window “other income” line; SimTower zeroes it on save (TOWER1) Inferred
12 i32 constructionCosts Finance window line; SimTower zeroes it on save (non-zero in TOWER1, 2, 5 and 6, zero after the game’s own re-save) Inferred
16 i32 lastQuarterMoney Finance window line; changes as the game runs Inferred
20 u16 tick 0 to 2,599 Time of day; changes as the game runs Known
22 i32 day Day counter, signed; changes as the game runs Known
26 u16 unknown 1 in TOWER2 only, else 0 Unknown
28 u16 lobbyHeight Ground lobby height in floors Inferred
30 u16 unknown 2 in the 4 and 5 star towers, 0xFFFF in the 3 star tower Star-related; could be a rating-state flag Unknown
32 u16 unknown 0xFFFF in all nine Unknown
34 u16 unknown 0xFFFF in all nine Unknown
36 u16 parkingColumnX 145, 145, 52, 52, 150, 150, 150, 0xFFFF, 117 (TOWER1 to MYTOWER order) The x of the parking ramp on B1, the one column every deeper ramp must sit under; 0xFFFF with no ramp. The game refuses a B1 ramp at another x, sets the word when the first ramp goes in and resets it to 0xFFFF when the only B1 ramp is bulldozed (runs on SimTower 1.0, 2026-11-15, Verticopolis/web#307) Known
38 u16 windowX Saved viewport X in world pixels Inferred
40 u16 windowY Saved viewport Y Inferred
42 u16 recyclingCount Recycling centers; equals the unit count in every save Known
44 u16 unknown 0 in all nine Unknown
46 u16 commercialCount Shops + restaurants + fast food; equals the unit count except TOWER3 (48 here, 38 units) Inferred
48 u16 securityCount Security offices (max 10) Known
50 u16 parkingStallCount All stalls, connected or not Known
52 u16 clinicCount Medical clinics; equals the clinic list count Known
54 u16 hallCinemaCount Party halls + cinemas (TOWER3: 12 here, 11 units) Inferred
56 u16 namedUnits Custom-named tenants Inferred
58 u16 namedPeople 0 in all nine Custom-named people Unknown
60 u16 unknown 0 Unknown
62 u32 bomb 0 Floor and position while a bomb event runs [TD] Unknown

Comparing each original with SimTower’s own re-save (TOWER1, 2, 5, 6, 7): only bytes 4 to 7 and 16 to 22 change because the game keeps running, bytes 12 to 15 (and 8 to 11 in TOWER1) are zeroed, and byte 2 (stars) never changes. No 1-star or 2-star save exists to test, so header 30 across those levels is still unchecked.

Open question: bytes 26, 30, 32, 34, 36, 44, 60 and the zero tail (66 to 559). TOWER3’s two count mismatches (46 and 54) suggest the counts are running counters the game does not always keep in step with the floor map.

3. Floors (120 records)

Section titled “3. Floors (120 records)”

Each floor: 6-byte header, n unit records of 18 bytes, a 188-byte remap.

Off Size Field Meaning Status
0 u16 n Unit records on this floor Known
2 u16 left Smallest left of the floor’s units Known
4 u16 right Largest right of the floor’s units Known
6 18 n units Unit records, ascending left Known
6 + 18 n 188 remap 94 x u16: logical unit id to list index. The game numbers a floor’s units and gives a plain floor tile (type 0) no id; our exporter numbers the units in record order, skipping plain floor tiles, and writes 0 past them (the game assigns ids in build order; unused entries are 0) Known

left and right match the units on every floor of every save. The remap maps the id that people records and retail rows use (“logical id”) to the unit’s position in the list; ids are unique per floor. A unit record carries its own id in byte 12. A party hall or theatre record in the tail names its halves by these ids, and the game crashed on a party hall whose record named ids that counted plain floor tiles (Verticopolis/web#1932), so the exporter skips them as the game does. The importer reads it to find the room a retail row describes (section 5).

3.1 Unit record (18 bytes)

Section titled “3.1 Unit record (18 bytes)”
Off Size Field Values and meaning Status
0 u16 left First tile Known
2 u16 right End tile, half open Known
4 u8 type Section 6 of tdt-format.md. As a signed byte, negative = under construction: 226 and 227 are a Party Hall under construction (-30, -29) in TOWER3 Known
5 u8 state Hotels: bits 3 and 4 stay state (1 checked in, 2 asleep, 3 vacant and clean; 0 is never written by the game), 0x20 dirty, 0x40 a flag the game keeps when it loads a file that has it but clears it from a vacant or dirty room (never found in a save of a tower played in the game; tdt-format.md section 5.1), low 2 bits guests inside now, which the game resets on load; the finance guest lines count rooms in stay state 1 or 2 at full complement (section 7.2). Offices and condos carry a nonzero value while occupied Known for hotels
6 u8 row Shops, restaurants and fast food: the index of the room’s row in the retail table (section 5), which names the room back, in every one of the seven observation saves (Verticopolis/web#2044). Party halls and theatres: the row of the party hall and theatre table (section 8) that holds the venue, the same in all its pieces. Byte 7 is 0 in all 561 retail rooms of the game’s saves we hold, none of which has more than 256; our exporter writes a retail row past 255 as a 16-bit number in bytes 6 and 7, low byte first, which a headless run of the game on a 280-room export confirmed (rooms 257 to 280 drew rows 256 to 279) Known for retail, Inferred for venues
7 u8 unknown 0 everywhere except parking stalls, where bytes 6 and 7 are one u16 (low byte first): the stall’s record in the stall table (section 8), so byte 7 is 1 exactly where that index is 256 or more. All 1,471 stalls in the nine saves point at a record naming their own floor and logical id (Verticopolis/web#307) Known for stalls
8 u32 first person High 16 bits always 0. The index of the unit’s first record in the people block (section 4); its people follow in a row. A party hall half keeps 40 and a theater screen piece (types 35 and 34) 56. A party hall without its people crashes the game a few seconds after loading (Verticopolis/web#1932) Inferred
12 u8 logical id The unit’s logical id on its floor (section 3), 255 on a plain floor tile, which has none Inferred
13 u8 flag 0 or 1 Unknown
14 u8 usually 1 Unknown
15 u8 state Theatres (all four parts): the film’s draw, 2 for a new film, then 1 and 0 as it ages. The census counts a theatre at 40 per step plus 40 (120, 80, 40) and picks up a changed draw the next morning (tdt-format.md section 11). Party halls: 255. Other kinds: 2 normal; 1, 0, 3 seen; 255 on vacant or non-applicable units Known for theatres
16 u8 rent 0 Very Low, 1 Low, 2 Average, 3 High, 4 none Inferred
17 u8 live value 0 for retail, hotels, lobby, security, housekeeping; small nonzero for offices and condos Unknown

Multi-story units are one record per floor (parts); the importer merges them.

Floor indexes run 0 to 119 (our floor is the index minus 9, see tdt-format.md section 5). Rows 110 to 119 hold nothing except the cathedral, which stands on rows 109 to 113 as five stacked parts (types 40 on floor 100 up to 36 on floor 104).

Rows that are not rooms. Type 0 is a paving piece (state 2) that fills floor tiles between rooms; type 45 is the metro tunnel, on floor index 0 only (our floor -9, B10), running along both sides of the station (TOWER7: tiles 0 to 208 and 238 to 375). A tunnel record has state 0 and no logical id (byte 12 FF), with the paving piece’s tail; the piece left of the station repeats the station’s bytes 8 to 11 (TOWER2, TOWER3 and TOWER7), which the exporter writes as zero (Verticopolis/web#1503). The metro station is three records, types 31, 32 and 33 on floor indexes 2, 1 and 0, 30 tiles wide each (TOWER7: tiles 208 to 238). Widths seen in the saves: security 16, medical center 26, housekeeping 15, recycling center 25, parking stall 4, parkade ramp 16 (type 44).

Parking rows hold stalls (type 11) and one ramp per segment (what the rules around them mean is in tdt-format.md section 6.1). A row may carry short paving pieces between stalls (TOWER7 B5: tiles 138 to 141 and 149 to 150, state 2), drawn flat grey with cars only in stalls; the file still counts those stalls as connected. A paving piece 56 tiles wide (TOWER1) leaves the stalls beyond it disconnected, as the file’s count says.

4. People (u32 N, then N records of 16)

Section titled “4. People (u32 N, then N records of 16)”

N is a pool size: 9,984 in TOWER2, always a multiple of 256 in game saves. Most slots are zero; in the nine saves 44,012 records are non-zero.

Off Size Field Meaning Status
0 u8 floor Home floor index (file index) Known
1 u8 unit Logical unit id on that floor (through the remap) Known
2 u8 slot Person number inside the unit Known
3 u8 zero 0 in every record Known
4 u8 unit type Type of the home unit Known
5 u8 flags 32 = belongs to a deleted unit [TD]; other bits not decoded Inferred
6 u8 state a Unknown
7 u8 current floor File index; negative means outside [OS] Inferred
8 to 13 6 B state Walking, waiting and goal state Unknown
14 u16 stress / evaluation Inferred

Check: for 40,122 of the 44,012 non-zero records the (floor, unit id) pair finds a unit of the stated type. The 3,890 mismatches are multi-part units: theatre screens (34 and 35 against the stated 18), party halls (30 against 29).

Our exporter writes N as the records in use plus at least 256 spare, rounded up to a multiple of 256. With no spare, the game crashed when the player built a theatre after loading the tower (Verticopolis/web#2044).

5. Retail table (512 rows of 18)

Section titled “5. Retail table (512 rows of 18)”

One row per shop, restaurant or fast-food unit. The row count equals the sum of those units in each save.

Off Size Field Meaning Status
0 u8 floor Floor index; 0xFF marks an empty row Known
1 u8 unit Logical unit id Known
2 to 5 4 B venue statistics Not decoded Unknown
6, 7 2 x u8 expected visitors Their sum is the visitors the room expects that day; it starts at 10 when a room opens and climbs by 5 to 15 a day to the cap (Verticopolis/web#1500) Observed
8 u8 served yesterday The visitors the room served the day before: its census. The finance block’s shop, fast food and restaurant lines are the sums of this byte by class Known
9, 10 2 B venue statistics Not decoded Unknown
11 u8 style The room’s style: 0 to 10 for shops, 0 to 3 for fast food and restaurants in the observation saves (Verticopolis/web#2044; list in tdt-format.md section 8). A headless run of one of our exports drew a Hamburger Stand (style 2) and a Sushi Bar (style 3) whose byte 6 held rows 5 and 6, past their style lists Known
12 u8 venue statistics Not decoded Unknown
13 u8 constant 255 in every row Known
14 to 17 4 B venue statistics Unknown

Byte 8 is pinned by the seven observation saves in conformance/fixtures/tdt-observed/ (the original game playing two of the Reddit towers forward, Verticopolis/web#1500): on every one, the sum of byte 8 per class equals the finance block’s line for shops, fast food and restaurants. The caps the original keeps are 25 a day for a shop (30 on a weekend) and 35 for fast food and restaurants (50 on a weekend).

6. Elevator slot (24 per file)

Section titled “6. Elevator slot (24 per file)”

An unbuilt slot is 194 bytes (the header). A built slot is the header plus:

Part Size Count
Per-floor map 480 (120 x 4) 1
Per-floor bytes 240 (2 x 120) 1
Floor entries 324 k
Car blocks 346 8

Total 194 + 720 + 324 k + 2,768. All 146 built shafts in the nine saves have eight cars, so a dependence of the car-block count on the header’s car byte is neither proven nor ruled out; [TD] says one block per car. Our codec always writes eight and the game loads it. The spec’s per-car size of 348 is wrong by two: a stride search over every shaft in the nine saves peaks at exactly 346 (16 + 168 + 42 + 120), and 720 + 8 x 346 equals the 3,140 + 348 that older versions of tdt-format.md described as a zero “fixed block”.

6.1 Header (194 bytes)

Section titled “6.1 Header (194 bytes)”
Off Size Field Values and meaning Status
0 u8 used 1 built Known
1 u8 type 0 express, 1 standard, 2 service Known
2 u8 capacity 42 or 21 seen; unreliable (a service shaft carried 21) Inferred
3 u8 cars 8 in all 146 built shafts Known
4 56 B schedule The Elevator window’s settings, four groups of 14 bytes (see below). Our exporter’s default is 01 x14, 05 x14, 00 x28; all zero = no cars run. An import keeps a block that differs from that default and an export writes it back (Verticopolis/web#305) Inferred
60 u8 shown 1 shown, 0 hidden [TD] Inferred
61 u8 reserved Unknown
62 u16 x Left tile Known
64 u8 topFloor File index Known
65 u8 bottomFloor File index Known
66 120 B serviced One byte per floor: 1 the shaft stops there Known
186 8 B homes Home floor of each car Inferred

The schedule block is four groups of 14 bytes, and each group is two runs of seven: bytes 0 to 6 of a group for weekdays and 7 to 13 for weekends. The Elevator window has six time periods a day (manual p.42 to 46), and the saves match: where a group differs from its default, it differs at positions 0 to 5 and 7 to 12 (for example 01 01 01 01 01 01 00 01 01 01 01 01 01 00) and positions 6 and 13 kept the group’s default. What the saves the game wrote (the Reddit saves and the observation saves) show:

Bytes Values seen Reading Status
0 to 13 1 in every shaft Unknown Unknown
14 to 27 5 mostly; also 1, 2, 3 and 10 Waiting Car Response, in floors (manual default 5) Inferred
28 to 41 0, 1, 2; weekdays often 1 2 1 0 2 0 Possibly the period’s direction mode (normal, express to top, express to bottom) Inferred
42 to 55 0 or 1 Unknown, possibly Standard Floor Departure Unknown

The original game loads our exports, which carry the default block, but no run has confirmed the readings above: they come from the values alone. Our simulation does not run these settings: it schedules cars by the hour (src/schedule.rs). So the importer keeps a non-default block on its shaft as tdtSchedule, a save carries it (version 13), and an export writes it back byte for byte. A shaft built in this game, or one whose block is the default or all zero, keeps nothing and exports the default.

6.2 Per-floor map (480 bytes, 120 x 4)

Section titled “6.2 Per-floor map (480 bytes, 120 x 4)”

One 4-byte entry for every floor index 0 to 119, whether or not the shaft spans it. Values seen in a standard shaft with floors 10 to 24 and in a 69-floor express:

Byte Content Status
0 On serviced stop floors an ordinal (1 on the ground lobby, 2, 3, 4, 5 on the next lobbies in order). 0x80 on the first floors above the ground lobby and 0x40 on the floor just below a sky lobby Unknown
1 to 3 Constant across all floors between two stops; changes at each stop. Seen: 00 00 00 39, 00 00 60 00, 00 00 1e 00, 00 c0 01 00 (a bit set that differs for each stretch between lobbies) Unknown

The pattern looks like a per-stretch bitmask (which local shafts serve the stretch) laid under the per-floor index. The meaning of the individual bits is not decoded.

6.3 Per-floor bytes (240 bytes, 2 x 120)

Section titled “6.3 Per-floor bytes (240 bytes, 2 x 120)”

Two arrays of 120 bytes. The first is zero in the sampled shafts; the second is nonzero only at lobby floors (values 3 to 8, for example 5 at index 24 and 7 at index 39 in TOWER4’s express). Not decoded.

6.4 Floor entry (324 bytes, k of them)

Section titled “6.4 Floor entry (324 bytes, k of them)”

k is every spanned floor for standard and service shafts, and only the eligible floors for express shafts (file index minus 9 is at most 1 or a multiple of 15). The “at most 1” part covers every basement floor, so the express shafts in the saves carry an entry for each underground floor. Layout from [TD], consistent with the nine saves:

Off Size Field Status
0 u8 people waiting up Inferred
1 u8 unknown Unknown
2 u8 people waiting down Inferred
3 u8 unknown Unknown
4 160 B 40 x u32 person indices, down Inferred
164 160 B 40 x u32 person indices, up Inferred

The index arrays are not cleared when a person boards, so stale values are normal (most entries carry about 40 non-zero bytes in saves from busy towers).

7.2 Finance (F+88, 132 bytes, 33 x i32)

Section titled “7.2 Finance (F+88, 132 bytes, 33 x i32)”

The hotel lines (single, twin, suite) are the number of rooms in stay state 1 or 2 (section 3.1, byte 5) times the complement 1, 2 and 2. Checked on all nine saves; TOWER2 gives 180, 242 and 81 rooms for lines of 180, 484 and 162.

Bytes Content Status
0 to 39 People per class: Office, Hotel single, Hotel twin, Hotel suite, Shop, Fast food, Restaurant, Party hall, Theatre, Condo Known
40 to 43 Total people (sum checked in all nine) Known
44 to 83 Income per class, same order Inferred
84 to 87 Total income (sum checked) Known
88 to 127 Expense: Lobby, Elevator, Express, Service, Escalator, Parking, Recycling, Metro, Housekeeping, Security Inferred
128 to 131 Total expense (sum checked) Known

7.3 Undecoded flags (F+220, 12 bytes)

Section titled “7.3 Undecoded flags (F+220, 12 bytes)”

00 00 FF FF FF FF 00 00 then four bytes each 0 or 1. Unknown.

7.4 Undecoded bytes (F+232, 42 bytes)

Section titled “7.4 Undecoded bytes (F+232, 42 bytes)”

Two single bytes, then two groups of ten bytes, then ten u16 values. Unknown; ten-wide groups suggest a per-security-office or per-clinic table.

7.5 Parking (F+274, 1,026 bytes)

Section titled “7.5 Parking (F+274, 1,026 bytes)”

u16 connected-stall count, then 512 x u16: the stall table records (section 8) of the connected stalls, ascending, the rest zero. Header byte 50 counts all stalls. Known. The game rebuilds this list from the stall table within a few game days, so the table, and each stall’s bytes 6 and 7, decide which stalls count; a list without the table comes back empty (Verticopolis/web#307). All nine reddit saves match: the count equals the stall total except TOWER1 (55 of 63, the stalls a whole row of paving away from their ramp are not counted). TOWER7 also holds two short paving pieces in one row, yet the file still counts every stall there as connected, which is why the importer closes such gaps (tdt-format.md section 6.1). Our own connectivity rule is in classic-parking-audit.md.

7.6 Clinic list (F+1,300, 22 bytes)

Section titled “7.6 Clinic list (F+1,300, 22 bytes)”

u16 count (equals header byte 52), then 10 x u16 clinic ids. Known.

7.7 Stairs (F+1,322, 640 bytes, 64 x 10)

Section titled “7.7 Stairs (F+1,322, 640 bytes, 64 x 10)”
Off Size Field Status
0 u8 built (1 in use) Known
1 u8 type (0 escalator, 1 stairs; 2 to 5 are multi-story types never seen) Known
2 u16 x (left tile) Known
4 u16 floor (file index of the lower floor) Known
6 i16 crowd going up (signed; 0xFFFB occurs) Inferred
8 i16 crowd going down Inferred

Flights are packed from slot 0 except that TOWER2 leaves slot 20 empty. Flight counts: TOWER1 1, TOWER2 30, TOWER3 29, TOWER4 29, TOWER5 7, TOWER6 64, TOWER7 64, TOWER8 0, MYTOWER 37.

8. Tail (17,242 bytes)

Section titled “8. Tail (17,242 bytes)”

Offsets from the end of the stairs table. The groups below tile the tail with no gaps (the sizes add up to 17,242).

Off Size Content Status
0 3,872 8 segments of 484 bytes: 4-byte header then 120 x 4 bytes. Segments 4 to 7 are empty (00 00 FF FF). Segments 0 to 3 hold per-floor 4-byte entries (0x86, 0x78 values) and a header like 00 01 0A 07 for the ground lobby Unknown
3,872 120 One flag byte per floor (values 1 to 3), clustered near lobbies and commercial floors Unknown
3,992 20 Ten security-office locators: floor index u8, logical unit id u8; FFFF empty Inferred
4,012 3,072 The stall table: 512 records of 6 bytes, one per stall: its floor index, its logical unit id on that floor, then a u32 (0 in our exports); empty FF 00 00 00 00 00. The game rebuilds the connected list (section 7.5) from this table, so a stall no record names is never connected (Verticopolis/web#307) Known
7,084 96 16 lobby records of 6 bytes: pad, 3-byte elevator bitmask, floor, pad; empty 00 00 00 00 FF 00; seven lobbies in the nine saves (floor indexes 10, 24, 39, 54, 69, 84, 99) Inferred
