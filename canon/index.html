Classic canon records

Dated research into how the 1994 game behaves, and the runs of the original on a PC that settle it. They are records, not live status: the rules themselves live in src/, and ../parity.md is the current checklist of how a Classic tower matches the original.

Page What it records
classic-shop-audit.md The shop, restaurant and entertainment rules against the public SimTower references (2026-10-30)
classic-parking-audit.md Parking and hotel suites: the claim under test, the sources and the PC experiments (2026-10-30)
classic-parity-pc-test-spec.md The approved original-game test scenarios, batch 1
classic-parity-pc-run-plan.md How that run is split and what each scenario showed

The 1994 save format has its own folder, ../tdt/. The PC harness that runs the original game is tools/simtower/ in Verticopolis/web.

These pages came from Verticopolis/web on 2026-11-20 with their history. Issue and pull request numbers (#N) in them are Verticopolis/web’s unless they name another repository.
